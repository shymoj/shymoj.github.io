Sony PlayStation 4 Pro Review!

Sony's PS4 Pro outputs a 4K and HDR signal compatible with high-end TVs, promising sharper visuals and increased frame rates for enhanced games. The console includes a 1TB hard drive and works with all PS4 games, apps, and accessories. One drawback is the lack of Ultra HD 4K Blu-ray disc support.

Microsoft Xbox One S Review!

Microsoft's Xbox One S is a sleek game console that is 40 percent smaller than the original and ditches the bulky external power brick. It plays 4K video from streaming services and Ultra HD Blu-rays, and supports HDR contrast on video and games. The updated controller adds Bluetooth support, though native 4K gaming is reserved for higher-tier hardware.
